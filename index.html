How it works

Kalshi runs a market every 15 minutes on whether Bitcoin (and Ethereum) will finish above a target price. With 1:00 left in each market, the bot checks three things:

  • The favorite costs between 65¢ and 85¢.
  • The gap between the buy and sell price is 5¢ or less.
  • The favorite's price has risen over the last 3 minutes.

If all three pass, it buys the favorite. In Paper mode it records one practice contract. In Live mode it spends your chosen share of the cash in Kalshi's crypto account (exchange index 2).

Tested on 10½ months of past markets, this won about 81% of the time and made roughly 3¢ per contract after fees. Each loss costs about four wins, so losing weeks happen. Past results do not guarantee future ones. Start in Paper mode.
