How to read this

  • Avg per contract: average profit or loss per contract, in cents, after the fee. Over many trades this is what matters, not the win rate: a strategy that wins 93% of the time at 95¢ can still lose money.
  • 95% range: the average plus or minus about two standard errors. Fewer than 30 settled trades is too few to judge.
  • Losing: the whole range is below zero. Ahead so far: the whole range is above zero. Can't tell yet: the range includes zero; the estimate of trades still needed assumes the average stays where it is.
  • "Bot's own rule" is the rule the bot uses for accounts in Live mode. Real-money trading is currently switched off for the whole bot.
  • Results come from the markets' official Kalshi outcomes, checked every 5 minutes. Days are Pacific time.
